Our honest review

Nigeria's oldest platform since 2008 but most expensive at ₦8/unit. Best for large organisations that prioritise reliability over cost. High minimum recharge of ₦5,000 may not suit small businesses.

Pricing in practice

Example: If you recharge ₦5,000 at ₦8 per SMS, you get 625 messages — e.g. one broadcast to 625 members or multiple smaller sends. At 1M+ units the rate drops to ₦6. Best suited when you need reliability and volume for large organisations.
