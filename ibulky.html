Pricing & plans

Volume-based tiered pricing; unit cost decreases at higher quantities. Minimum deposit ₦1,000; minimum purchase 50 units. Units never expire.

Pricing in practice

Example: If you recharge ₦1,000 and buy in the 50–5,000 unit tier (₦1.80 per unit), you get about 555 messages — enough for one blast to 555 contacts or several smaller campaigns. At higher volumes the rate drops (e.g. ₦1.30 at 1M+ units), and units never expire so you can use them over time.

Platform features & technical specs

  • API: Secure, SSL-encrypted RESTful API for websites and custom applications.
  • Messaging: Concatenated SMS (up to 3,000+ characters), Personalized SMS (e.g. [NAME] tags), Branded SMS, Flash SMS, Scheduled SMS.
  • Campaign management: Auto Requeue for recurring messages (e.g. birthday reminders); tag-based personalization.
  • HLR Lookup: Validates mobile numbers (active, disabled, roaming) to clean contact lists and improve delivery.
  • Contact management: Grouping, import/export in .csv, .xml, .html, .txt.
  • Reporting: Real-time transaction logs and delivery reports via platform and API.
