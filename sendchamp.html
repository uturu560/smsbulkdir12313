Pricing in practice

Example: SMS at ₦6 per message means ₦6,000 gives you 1,000 SMS — e.g. order alerts or promos to 1,000 contacts. WhatsApp is ₦2.70 per message, so the same ₦6,000 buys about 2,222 WhatsApp messages. You pay per channel, so you can mix SMS and WhatsApp depending on what each campaign needs.
